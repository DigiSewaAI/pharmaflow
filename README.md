## About PharmaFlow

PharmaFlow is a complete pharmacy management system built with **Laravel 13**. It helps pharmacies manage medicines, inventory, sales, purchases, customers, suppliers, and generate reports efficiently.

### Key Features

- **Medicine Management** – CRUD, search, filter, import/export (Excel)
- **Inventory Management** – Stock in/out, batch tracking, expiry alerts
- **Point of Sale (POS)** – Quick billing, discount/tax, invoice PDF
- **Purchase Management** – Supplier orders, payment tracking
- **Customer & Supplier Management** – Loyalty points, outstanding balance
- **Reports & Analytics** – Revenue, sales, profit, stock reports with charts
- **Role-based Access** – Owner, Admin, Staff with permissions
- **Multi-tenant Ready** – Supports multiple pharmacies (future)
